Firmware 2.1 (10 November 2026)

- Focus now returns to infinity when you switch to MF.
- Proper support for the camera's sleep mode.
- More reliable start-up and shutdown, and faster shutdown.
- More robust communication with the camera.

Known issues:
- Does not yet work on 4th-gen bodies (A7m4, A7m5, A7C2, A7Cr, A7r4, etc.)
- AF-S is still unusable: it misses focus.
- AF tuning is for 50 mm lenses only.

==== Previous versions ====

Firmware 2.0 (4 November 2026)

- The adapter now identifies itself as a native E-mount lens.
- Autofocus now works in video mode.
- Improved AF-C range and tracking.

I hope you enjoy this community firmware.
